UniNotes Support

Something broken, confusing, or missing? Email phoenixriseinteractive@gmail.com. It goes to the person who built the app, and you'll usually hear back within a couple of days.

When you write, it helps to include your device (e.g. iPad Air, iPhone 15) and what you were doing when it went wrong. A screenshot is gold.

Common questions

My term starts on a different date

Go to Reviews → Adjust and nudge the start date a week either way. Every week in the year shifts with it. You can also start a fresh year from Settings → Start new academic year.

My university isn't listed

Pick your country and the closest match, then set the start date yourself on the confirm screen. If you tell us your university and its term dates we'll add it.

Where are my notes stored? Are they backed up?

On your device only. There's no account and nothing in the cloud. If your device is backed up (iCloud or Google backup), the app's data is included in that backup. Deleting the app deletes your notes.

How do I send a note to a friend?

Open the note, tap the share icon, and choose Send to UniNotes. Send the file however you like — WhatsApp, AirDrop, Messages. When your friend taps it, it opens straight into their UniNotes in the same week and module. Send as text sends a plain copy for people who don't have the app.

A to-do I wrote in a future week isn't on my list

It's there — look for "N coming up" under this week's list, or tap See all. To-dos written in past weeks that aren't ticked show as overdue.

I want to start over

Settings → Start over clears everything and returns you to setup. This can't be undone.

Feature requests

Genuinely welcome. UniNotes is built by one person who uses it, and the best ideas so far have come from other students. Email them in.
