Mad Atom Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

The short version: Mad Atom has no account and no sign in. Your progress lives on your device. The only ads are ones you choose to watch, and those come from Google AdMob, which collects some data to show them. We never sell anything about you.

Who we are

Mad Atom is made by Phoenix Rise Interactive, an independent game studio based in the United Kingdom. For anything in this policy, contact us at phoenixriseinteractive@gmail.com.

What the game stores, and where

Mad Atom stores your progress, unlocked cards and heroes, settings and a simple log of your games on your device only. There is no account, no sign in and no cloud sync. None of this is sent to us. If you delete the game, this data is deleted with it.

Ads (only when you choose)

Mad Atom never forces an ad on you. Sometimes it offers one, for example "watch an ad to retry with weaker aliens". If you say yes, the ad is shown by Google AdMob. We ask Google for non-personalised ads only, rated PG or lower, so ads are not targeted at you and you are not tracked across other apps. To show and measure ads, Google may still collect:

  • a device identifier (such as the advertising identifier, if your phone allows it),
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address,
  • information about the ad, such as whether it was shown, watched or tapped,
  • diagnostic information, such as crashes and load times of the ad.

This data goes to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information in ads. On iPhone you can limit ad tracking in Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking. If you never tap an ad offer, no ad is loaded on screen.

Game updates

Mad Atom checks for new content (new levels, fixes) by downloading files from GitHub. Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address when the game asks for those files. The game sends nothing else with the request.

In app purchases (not yet)

This version of Mad Atom has no in app purchases. If a future version adds optional purchases, payment will be handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms. We would never see your card details or account information. We will update this page before that happens.

What Mad Atom does not do

  • No accounts, chat or messaging.
  • No analytics of our own, and no tracking across other apps.
  • No forced ads, and no ads between levels.
  • No selling or renting of data.

Children

Mad Atom is a chemistry game made for players aged 13 and over. It has no accounts, no chat and no way to share personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. We hold none: your progress is on your device, and deleting the game deletes it. For ad data, see Google's privacy tools at myadcenter.google.com. If you believe we hold something about you, for example from an email you sent us, write to phoenixriseinteractive@gmail.com and we will deal with it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we will update this page first and change the effective date at the top.
