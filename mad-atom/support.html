Mad Atom Support

Something broken, confusing, or too hard? Email phoenixriseinteractive@gmail.com. It goes to the person who built the game, and you will usually hear back within a couple of days.

When you write, it helps to include your device (for example iPhone 15) and what you were doing when it went wrong: which experiment, which alien. A screenshot is gold.

Common questions

I'm stuck on an experiment

Every alien is weak to some kinds of chemicals and shrugs off others. Watch which cards hurt it most and change your mix. If you lose, the game may offer a retry with weaker aliens.

An ad didn't play, did I lose my reward?

No. If an ad can't load (no internet, nothing to show), Mad Atom gives you the reward anyway.

The screen effects are too much

Open the pause menu and turn on Reduce flashing. It calms the screen shake and the trippy effects.

Will I lose my progress if I get a new phone?

Progress is stored on the device. If your phone is backed up with iCloud, the game's data comes with it when you restore to a new phone.

I want to start over

Deleting the game and reinstalling it clears all progress.

Ideas and new aliens

Genuinely welcome. Mad Atom is built by one person. Email ideas in.
