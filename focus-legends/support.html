Focus Legends Support

Something broken, confusing, or missing? Email phoenixriseinteractive@gmail.com. It goes to the person who built the game, and you'll usually hear back within a couple of days.

When you write, it helps to include your device (e.g. iPhone 15, Samsung Galaxy S24) and what you were doing when it went wrong. A screenshot is gold.

Common questions

I bought something and it hasn't unlocked

Open the game's settings and tap Restore purchases. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the receipt Apple or Google sent you and we'll sort it.

I want a refund

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple and Google, not by us. On iOS, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, and choose the purchase.

Will I lose my progress if I get a new phone?

Progress is stored on the device. If your device is backed up (iCloud or Google backup), the game's data is included when you restore to a new phone. Purchases are tied to your App Store or Google Play account and can always be restored.

I want to start over

Deleting the game and reinstalling it clears all progress. Purchases can be restored afterwards.

Feature requests

Genuinely welcome. Focus Legends is built by one person who uses it. Email ideas in.
