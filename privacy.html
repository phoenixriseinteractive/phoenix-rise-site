Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Applies to UniNotes and Focus Legends

The short version: our apps don't have accounts, don't show ads, don't track you, and don't sell anything about you. Your notes and your progress live on your device. The only thing that ever leaves it is an anonymous crash report if the app crashes.

Who we are

Phoenix Rise Interactive is an independent app studio based in the United Kingdom. We make UniNotes and Focus Legends. For anything in this policy, contact us at phoenixriseinteractive@gmail.com.

UniNotes

What the app stores, and where

UniNotes stores everything on your device only:

  • Your university, course and year of study, which you pick during setup.
  • The academic year the app builds from that: semesters, weeks and their dates.
  • Your modules, notes, to-dos, highlights and any photos you add to notes.
  • When you last reviewed each note.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. We cannot see it, recover it or hand it to anyone.

Photos

If you add a photo to a note, the app asks for permission to read your photo library and copies only the photo you chose into the app's own storage. It never scans or uploads your library.

Sharing a note

When you share a note, the app hands it to the app you pick — Messages, WhatsApp, AirDrop, Mail and so on. From that point the note is handled by that app under its own privacy policy. We don't see it and it doesn't pass through any server of ours.

Term start dates

The app keeps a record of the term start date it suggested and the one you chose, so it can make better suggestions. This record contains no personal information — only the university, course category, year of study and the two dates — and it stays on your device.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a service that helps us find and fix bugs. A report contains the type of error, where in the app's code it happened, your device model, operating system version and the app version. It does not contain your notes, your course details, your name, your email, or any identifier that links it to you. Reports are kept for 90 days. Sentry's own policy is at sentry.io/privacy.

What UniNotes does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs other than the crash reporter above.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data — there is nothing to sell.

Focus Legends

Focus Legends stores your progress, settings and session history on your device only. It has no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync. It does not collect analytics, does not show advertising and does not send anything to us. Deleting the app deletes your data.

In-app purchases

Focus Legends offers optional in-app purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your card details, billing address or Apple/Google account information — we only learn that a purchase was made so the app can unlock what you bought. Purchases are tied to your App Store or Google Play account, and can be restored on a new device from within the app.

Children

UniNotes is for university and college students and is not directed at children under 13.

Focus Legends can be used by anyone, including children under 13. It is built with that in mind: it has no account, no chat, no advertising, no analytics and no way to share personal information. Purchases go through Apple or Google, which apply their own parental controls and family settings — parents can require approval for every purchase in the device's Screen Time (iOS) or Family Link (Android) settings. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. If you believe a child has sent us personal information — for example by email — contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. Because we hold none, the practical answer is simple: everything is on your device, under your control, and deleting the app deletes it. If you believe we hold something about you — for example from an email you sent us — write to phoenixriseinteractive@gmail.com and we'll deal with it promptly.

Changes to this policy

Neither app has accounts today. We may add optional accounts or cloud sync in the future — for example so you can back up notes or keep progress across devices. If we do, it will be optional, we'll update this page first and change the effective date at the top, and the app will tell you exactly what an account stores before you create one. Nothing you have on your device today will be uploaded without you choosing it.

New apps from Phoenix Rise Interactive will be added to this policy as they're released.
